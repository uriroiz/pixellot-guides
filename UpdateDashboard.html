Export and Manage Data in Pixellot CSM Dashboard

Creation Date: February 22, 2026 Created By: Uri Roiz

View on Tango

View most recent version on Tango.ai

Summary: This guide shows how to export event data from the Power BI Customer Dashboard as CSV files (per year), then upload those files into the client's Production Hours in CSM Dashboard 2.0. Part 1: export 2024, 2025, and 2026 data from Power BI. Part 2: sign in to CSM Dashboard 2.0, edit the client, and upload the CSVs in Production Hours – Sport Breakdown.
